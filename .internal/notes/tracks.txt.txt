Messaging & News
Apps in scope: Telegram (accounts, bots, channels, Mini Apps), Discord, Slack. See new messages only from the chats you pick, get a short digest of the Telegram channels and crypto communities you follow, reply by voice or approve a reply drafted by an AI agent. Build the core on these apps; any other tools on top are fine. Prize: 1st $1,250 + G2 (1-2 pairs), 2nd $375.

Crypto & DeFi
Apps in scope: Gram Wallet, Polymarket, Hyperliquid, Trust Wallet. Check a wallet balance, live odds or an open position; place a testnet bet or order by voice and confirm with one tap. Demos must use testnet, paper trading or read-only data. Build the core on these apps; any other tools on top are fine. Prize: 1st $1,250 + G2 (1-2 pairs), 2nd $375.

Exchanges & Stocks
Apps in scope: Binance, OKX, Bybit, Coinbase, Interactive Brokers. A "trading session" app: PnL, positions and price alerts while it is open, and quick confirmations like "Move $500 from spot to margin? Yes / No". Demos must use testnet, paper trading or read-only data. Build the core on these apps; any other tools on top are fine. Prize: 1st $1,250 + G2 (1-2 pairs), 2nd $375.

AI Assistants & Work
Apps in scope: Claude, ChatGPT, OpenRouter, Notion, Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Drive). A short brief before your next meeting, your inbox cleared in 30 seconds, answers from your own notes; reply to an email, add a task or move a meeting by voice. Build the core on these apps; any other tools on top are fine. Prize: 1st $1,250 + G2 (1-2 pairs), 2nd $375.
