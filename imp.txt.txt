Overview
Even Realities × TVM Ventures × Daos Hub

Read a new Telegram message, check who you are meeting next, get a short answer from your AI. Even G2 puts a private display in everyday eyewear, so all of this takes a glance instead of your phone.

Heads Up is a 7-day online hackathon. Take the services people already use every day, starting with Telegram, and make them work on G2.

At a glance
Build week: Nov 23–29, 2026 · online, open to builders worldwide
Prizes: $10,000 prize pool: 10 winners + a Community Partner Award of up to $500, plus up to 10 pairs of G2
Fast-track: finalists pitch to TVM Ventures and partner VCs
Demo Day: Dec 3, 14:00–18:00 · Daos Hub, Dubai · finalists can demo remotely
No glasses needed: build and test in the free Even Hub simulator
Coming to Demo Day in Dubai? RSVP on Luma

What makes a great G2 app
It shows one thing that matters, then lets you act on it in seconds: reply by voice, confirm with a tap on the glasses or the R1 ring. We call this read and write, and it is what we judge first.

Tracks
Pick a main track and build your core integration on its apps: they all have open APIs, so every team can build a real, working integration. On top of that, you are free to add any other tools, like an LLM for reply drafts or speech-to-text. If your app genuinely connects apps from several tracks (e.g. Telegram + Hyperliquid in one flow), you can also enter a second track (up to 2).

01 · Messaging & News
Apps: Telegram (accounts, bots, channels, Mini Apps) · Discord · Slack

See new messages only from the chats you pick, for example your work group
Get a short digest of the Telegram channels and crypto communities you follow
Reply by voice, or approve a reply drafted by an AI agent
02 · Crypto & DeFi
Apps: Gram Wallet · Polymarket · Hyperliquid · Trust Wallet

Get a short brief before your next meeting
Clear your inbox in 30 seconds or get an answer from your own notes

G2 is the display, voice and input layer. Trading, custody and licensing stay with the underlying providers, reached through their APIs.

Build for G2
The hardware and your app
Display: monochrome green, 576 × 288 per eye
Input: microphone, touch controls on the glasses, R1 ring
Not on the glasses: camera, speaker
Your app: a web app (HTML, CSS, JavaScript or TypeScript) running in a WebView inside the Even Realities App, talking to G2 over Bluetooth through the Even Hub SDK
Know the limits before you design
One app on screen at a time. While your app is open, it can show live data and take input. When the user switches away, it cannot show anything or pop up alerts.
No access to other apps' notifications. Talk to the service directly through its API.
Bring your own speech-to-text. The SDK gives you raw microphone audio: send it to your own server (e.g. Whisper).
Use a backend. External calls must be whitelisted in app.json and pass CORS. Route API calls through your own server and never ship API keys inside the app.
Sign-in: Google sign-in does not work inside the WebView. Use a separate login page or a pairing code.
Design for a glance: one thing on screen and a few words. Think "trading session" or "inbox in 30 seconds", not dashboards.

See what already exists
Browse the Even Hub catalogue before you pick an idea: messages, news, AI assistants, navigation, timers. Build something new, or something clearly better. Even Realities will share tips on what makes a G2 app successful at kickoff.

Set up in 5 steps
Read the Even Hub docs.
Install Node.js and the Even tools:
   npm install -g @evenrealities/evenhub-cli
   npm install -g @evenrealities/evenhub-simulator
Start from the official minimal template and point the simulator at your local dev server.
Change one line of text and check that one input works before you build the full project.
Have G2? Install the Even Realities App, pair and update the glasses, and enable developer access on the portal to test on the device.
Submission and rules
What to submit
A public GitHub/GitLab repository with a README that explains setup
A demo video of up to 3 minutes, running in the simulator or on G2
A short description: the problem, who it is for, how it works, and the track
Optional: feature requests, up to 5 things the APIs you used were missing. We'll pass them to the teams behind those apps
Optional: a packaged .ehpk build
Rules
Teams of 1 to 4 people, from anywhere in the world. You can join one team only. A team can submit up to 2 projects, but we judge how production-ready an app is, so we recommend focusing on one.
Build during the hackathon. Open-source libraries and existing APIs are allowed; say in your README what existed before Nov 23.
Build your core integration on the apps listed in your track. On top of that, you can add any other tools (for example an LLM for reply drafts or speech-to-text). Entering a second track is optional.
Money safety: demos use testnet, paper trading or read-only data. Do not hold user funds. No financial advice. Never commit API keys or secrets.
Respect the terms of the services you integrate and the privacy of the people whose messages you show.
Submit on this page before the deadline. Organizers may disqualify projects that break these rules or the DoraHacks terms.
Judging
Usefulness on G2 · 30%. Solves a real everyday moment, works at a glance and hands-free.
Read and write · 25%. Acting is fast: a short phrase, one tap or the ring, with smart defaults or AI drafts instead of long dictation.
Technical execution · 20%. Works end to end, reliable, clean code.
UX for display and voice · 15%. Clear on a 576 × 288 monochrome display, natural by voice.
Business potential and safety · 10%. Real users and demand, careful with data and money.
Judges come from Even Realities, TVM Ventures, partner venture funds, sponsors and Daos Hub.
